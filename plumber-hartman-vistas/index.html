Softer water, different priorities

An average near 5 grains per gallon is the lowest in the Town's table. Scale still forms, but more slowly than in the 12 grain areas. Some households here may find they need less water treatment than neighbors elsewhere in Marana.

Before buying a softener, test your own water. The Town notes that you should check which unit a softener uses, ppm or grains per gallon. Our water softener and filtration page walks through the choices.

Problems that soft water does not explain

That points to an aerator or cartridge at that fixture rather than the whole supply.
