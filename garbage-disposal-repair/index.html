Leaking or jammed disposal: stop the power first

Water under the sink next to an electric motor is a hazard, and a jammed blade can injure a hand. Our 24/7 line can walk you through the safe steps while you wait for a visit.

  1. Never put your hand or any tool inside the grinding chamber, even if the unit seems off.
  2. Turn the disposal wall switch off, then switch off its breaker at the panel.
  3. Unplug the unit if it uses a cord under the sink, only if the cabinet floor is dry.
  4. Close the sink's water supply stops if water is running into the cabinet.
  5. Place a pan under the leak and call (855) 575-2890.

What your disposal is doing, and what it usually means

The sound and the leak location narrow it down quickly.

Humming but not turning

The motor has power but the blades are stuck. A bone, fruit pit or cap can jam it. Do not keep flipping the switch.

Silence when switched on

No hum and no spin can mean a tripped reset, a dead motor or a power problem. Check the red reset button on the bottom.

Leaking from the bottom

Water from the housing or the reset button area usually means the internal seal has failed. That is typically the end of the unit.

Leaking at the top or sides

The flange, drain gasket or sink connection may be loose. Those are repairable.

Slow drain or gurgling

Grease and food residue clog the trap or drain arm. The disposal itself may be fine.

Jams, hums and what is safe to try

Most jams free up with the manual wrench slot on the underside of the unit. With the power off, turn it back and forth until the blades move. Then press the reset button and test it with cold water running.

Do not use chemical drain cleaner in a jammed disposal. It sits in the chamber and can splash when a plumber opens it. If a few minutes with the wrench does not free the blades, call us.

The dishwasher line and the sink drain

A disposal has a knockout plug that must be removed before the dishwasher drain hose connects. If someone forgot, the dishwasher will back up into the sink. If we reconnect a hose, we also check the high loop or air gap that keeps dirty water from running back.

When a dishwasher leaves standing water and the sink also drains slowly, the line beyond the trap may be partly blocked. In that case our drain cleaning service clears the kitchen line.

Hard water and the desert kitchen

Marana Water's published figures range from roughly 3 to 14 grains per gallon depending on the neighborhood, and the Town says it poses no health risk. Scale does build up on seals, the drain flange and the inside of the housing, and mineral crust can make the grinder rough.

Cold water running during use helps flush waste and keeps grease from coating the pipe. Run the unit and flush with water for a few seconds after each use.

Replacement is usually a same-visit job when the sink and mounting ring are sound.
