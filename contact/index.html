If water is running right now

Protect the house first, then call. Water damage grows by the minute, and a few quick actions often save the most.

  1. Close the main water valve.
  2. Turn off the water heater power or gas control if the tank is leaking.
  3. Stay away from outlets, cords and the electrical panel if there is standing water.
  4. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and the gas utility before you call us.

What to have ready when you call

A few details help us understand the problem quickly. None of them is required, so call even if you do not have all of them.

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the problem is, such as the kitchen, a bathroom, the garage or the yard.
  • What you see, hear or smell, such as a hissing sound, a warm floor spot or a backed-up drain.
  • Whether the water is shut off and whether the problem has stopped.
  • The brand and age of a water heater, if the problem involves one.
  • A good callback number in case the call drops.
