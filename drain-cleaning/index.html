When a clogged drain is flooding your home

If water is rising in a tub, spilling from a sink, or bubbling up from a floor drain, call (855) 575-2890 right away. We take emergency calls 24/7. Until help arrives, the goal is to stop adding water and keep it off your floors.

  1. Stop using every sink, shower, dishwasher and washing machine in the house.
  2. Skip chemical drain openers. They sit in standing water and can burn skin or splash on whoever works on the line next.
  3. Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the wet area and stay clear of outlets near standing water.
  4. Note which fixtures gurgle or back up. If the tub backs up when the washer drains, the blockage is probably past the branch lines.
  5. Keep children and pets out of the wet area. Backed-up drain water carries bacteria.

Kitchen sink clogs: grease, scale and the dishwasher connection

Kitchen lines in Marana fight two things at once. Cooking fat cools inside the pipe and coats the walls, and hard water leaves mineral scale that makes those walls rough. Local water runs roughly 3 to 14 grains per gallon depending on the neighborhood, so ask your water provider where your address falls.

That roughness matters for your sink. Food scraps and grease catch on scale and build a plug that grows slowly, which is why a kitchen sink often gets slower for weeks before it stops.

The dishwasher and garbage disposal usually share the same trap and drain arm. If both back up into the sink, the blockage sits below where they join, and a plunger will not reach it.

Signs the clog is more than a hair in the trap

A single slow sink usually has a local cause. These patterns point deeper into the system.

Gurgling after you flush

Air is being pushed through water in a partly blocked line. The toilet and nearby drains share the same path.

Tub fills when the washer drains

The washer drain joins a larger line that is already restricted, so water finds the lowest opening.

Every fixture drains slowly

When all drains slow down together, the blockage is probably in a shared line or the main.

A sewer smell with no clear source

A dry trap, a vent problem or a partly blocked line can all let gas into the room.

It clogs again every few weeks

Snaking opened a hole but the pipe wall is still coated, or roots or damage are involved.

Drain snaking and cabling, and what the cable cannot do

Snaking, also called cabling, pushes a spinning steel cable through the pipe until the head breaks into the blockage. A small hand auger handles a sink trap or a tub. A drum machine with a longer, stiffer cable reaches branch lines and the run toward the main.

The cable bores a channel through the clog. It does not scrub the pipe wall, so grease and scale can remain behind and narrow the pipe again. That is why a kitchen line may clog again within weeks even after a clean snaking.

When that pattern shows up, we move to hydro jetting, which washes the wall with high-pressure water. If we suspect a crack or a root, a camera inspection comes first so we know what the pipe looks like before pushing anything through it.

Slab homes, roots and monsoon debris

Most newer Marana homes are built on concrete slabs, so drain lines often run in or under the slab. You cannot dig to a clog the way you might in a house with a crawlspace. That makes cleanouts and cables the main way in, and it is another reason repeat clogs deserve a camera look.

Roots are a general risk anywhere trees and shrubs sit near buried lines. They enter at joints and cracks and then catch paper and grease. If the problem reaches the lateral between the house and the street, our sewer line cleaning work covers that run.

Monsoon season runs June 15 to September 30. Heavy rain washes silt, leaves and grit toward yard and patio drains and open cleanouts, so check them before the storms and again after.

Shower and tub drains

Hair and soap scum are the usual culprits in a tub or shower. Hard water makes soap form sticky curds that cling to the pipe, so the blockage is often a mat of hair held together by residue. A pop-up stopper or strainer assembly can hold more than you expect, and cleaning it by hand is the first move before any cable goes in.

A drain line that leaks instead of clogging

Not every drain problem is a blockage. A damp cabinet floor, a stain on the ceiling below a bathroom, or a musty smell can point to a leaking drain joint or a cracked line. Under a slab, a drain leak may show up only as a recurring clog or a faint odor.

We check by running water and watching, by camera where the line is reachable, and by looking for the pattern in where it appears. Out toward Picture Rocks, we work in unincorporated Pima County, where repair permits come from the county rather than the Town. Ask the right office before any pipe is opened.

Drain lines in Marana homes are laid out under the 2024 International Plumbing Code (IPC) the Town has adopted, which is why vents and cleanouts matter: a plumber who knows where they belong can clear a line without cutting into it.
