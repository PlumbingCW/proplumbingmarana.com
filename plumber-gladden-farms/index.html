What the Town of Marana says about Gladden Farms

The Town marked the 20th anniversary of Gladden Farms in September 2024 with an event at Compass Park on North Tangerine Road. The land was farmland owned by Stanley Gladden until it was sold in 2002 to Crown West Land Group, and the first homes were built in 2004.

That timeline has a plain plumbing meaning. The earliest homes are now around two decades old, so a builder-installed water heater, toilet fill valve or faucet cartridge may be near the end of a normal service life. Newer phases will be younger. Your own paperwork or the heater label tells you which applies.

Living with about 9 grains per gallon

Nine grains per gallon is the Town's average for this system. It is softer than the 12 grains shown for the Picture Rocks and Saguaro Bloom side of town, and harder than the roughly 5 shown for Hartman Vistas. It is not extreme, but it adds up over years.

General plumbing advice, not a Gladden Farms finding: mineral scale settles on the bottom of a tank heater and crusts faucet aerators. A heater that rumbles while heating, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, can point to sediment. Our water heater repair page covers what can be fixed, and water heater replacement covers when a new tank is the better call.

Three quick checks for a Gladden Farms home

Read the heater label

Find the manufacture date. Tank heaters are commonly said to last roughly 8 to 12 years, which is general industry guidance and varies with the model and the water.

Listen to the toilets

A toilet that hisses or refills on its own is wasting water. Toilet repair is often a small fix when it is caught early.

Look at the aerators

White crust on a faucet tip is scale. Weak flow at one tap often points to the aerator or cartridge, not the supply line.

Permits and who to ask

Gladden Farms is inside the Town of Marana. Marana lists water heater replacement under its permit application process, with a safety inspection. Confirm the details with Development Services at (520) 382-2600 before work starts. Plumbing work in the Town follows the 2024 International Plumbing Code with local amendments (Town Code 7-1-2).
