Plumbing problems tend to show up as a symptom first. A warm floor, a puddle by the water heater or a drain that gurgles all point to different causes. The cards below group the work by problem, and each page explains the signs, the usual causes and what drives the cost.

If water is running or a drain is backing up, start with burst pipe repair, leak detection or sewer line cleaning. If you have time to plan, pages such as water heater installation and repiping help you compare options.

Most newer Marana homes sit on concrete slabs, so supply and drain lines often run in or under the slab or through attics. Water hardness also ranges from roughly 3 to 14 grains per gallon by neighborhood. Both facts shape which service fits, and the pages explain how.

How to choose where to start

Start with what you can see. A moving meter with everything off points to leak detection. A drain that backs up into the tub or washer suggests the main line, and a camera look through sewer camera inspection shows why.

If you are unsure, call and describe it. Phone help is part of the job, and we will say which page or service fits. Homes in Picture Rocks follow Pima County permit rules, so we mention that when it applies.

Work that touches gas, permits or the sewer main can involve other offices. We name them on the phone so you are not surprised later.
