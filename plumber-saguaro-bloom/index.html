New home, hard water

About 12 grains per gallon sits toward the harder end of the Town's table, where the airport system reads 13 to 15. Scale does not wait for a home to age. It starts coating heating surfaces and fixtures from the first month.

General advice: a tankless heater has a heat exchanger that scale can coat, and manufacturers usually call for periodic descaling. Check your owner's manual. Our tankless water heater page explains the basics, and water softeners cover the hard water side.

First-year habits for a new Saguaro Bloom home
