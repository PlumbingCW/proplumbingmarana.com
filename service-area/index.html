Plumbing Service Areas near Marana, AZ

We serve Marana and nearby towns from our base at 3864 W Tangerine Rd. This page covers the communities inside the Town, the unincorporated area around Picture Rocks and the ZIP codes that go with them.

Water systems, sewer providers and permit offices change from one neighborhood to the next, so each section ends with what that means for your plumbing.

Gladden Farms

Gladden Farms is a master-planned community with first homes from 2004 and more than 3,000 homes today. It has a Fry's grocery store and a shopping center of its own. The area is served by Marana Municipal water (system 10-150), with hardness of 6 to 12 grains per gallon and an average near 9.

At about 9 grains per gallon, scale builds in tank heaters and on faucet aerators over the years. Homes of this age may be on a first or second heater, so a quick look at the label tells you where you stand. Our pages on water heater replacement and water softeners explain the choices.

Continental Ranch and Continental Reserve

Continental Ranch was annexed by Marana in 1987 and has about 4,000 HOA homes near the Santa Cruz River corridor and Cortaro Road. Building began after 1992, so homes run from the 1990s onward. Its Continental Reserve area sits on the Picture Rocks water system (10-092) at about 12 grains per gallon.

In homes that have been standing for a few decades, supply shutoff valves, angle stops and older fixtures wear out. If a valve will not fully close, that is a good time to look at faucet repair or toilet repair before something fails at night. Sewer service for Continental Reserve and Cortaro comes from Pima County Regional Wastewater Reclamation at (520) 724-6609.

Dove Mountain in the Tortolita foothills

Dove Mountain is a master-planned resort community that opened in 1998, with about 5,000 homes in the Tortolita foothills northeast of the Town center. The Town's hardness table does not list it, so we do not quote a figure. Confirm your water provider and hardness on your bill.

A drop in pressure or a climbing bill in any home can point to the water service line, which is the pipe between the meter and the house. Leak detection helps locate the break.

Saguaro Bloom

Saguaro Bloom is a D.R. Horton new-construction community in northwest Marana, still under development in 2026. Homes run from 1,310 to 2,519 square feet, and the ZIP code is 85653. Water comes from the Airline/Lambert system (10-138) at about 12 grains per gallon, and Marana Water Reclamation handles sewer.

New homes have new heaters, but hard water starts working from the first day. Flushing the tank on a schedule and checking the pressure relief valve are habits worth starting early. If something does fail, the water heater repair page lists the usual causes.

Picture Rocks, outside the Town limits

Picture Rocks is a census-designated place in unincorporated Pima County, with its own page at emergency plumber Picture Rocks. Its primary ZIP is 85743, which the Postal Service labels Tucson, and part of the area is also in 85653. Permits there come from Pima County, not the Town of Marana.

Hardness on the Picture Rocks system averages about 12 grains per gallon. Your provider may differ by address, so confirm it on your water bill.

Other places we hear from

We take calls from Rillito, a separate community surrounded by Marana, as well as Cortaro, Twin Peaks, Hartman Vistas, Oasis Hills, San Lucas, Linda Vista and Tortolita. The water table above shows which Town system serves several of those areas.

Roads help place you. Tangerine Road, Thornydale Road, Silverbell Road, Avra Valley Road, Twin Peaks Road and Cortaro Road all cross Marana. Interstate 10 exits 236, 240, 242, 244 and 246 mark the Town's main interchanges.

ZIP codes we serve and ZIP codes nearby

ZIP codes we serve are 85653 and 85658. The 85653 code covers Marana, Avra Valley, Saguaro Bloom and Town Hall, and 85658 includes the Tangerine corridor and our office.

Nearby ZIP codes include 85743 for Picture Rocks, 85741 and 85742 for Tucson, and 85654 for Rillito. Call (855) 575-2890 with your address if you are unsure.

Sewer lines belong to two owners

The Town is responsible for sewer mains in Town rights of way and easements, while the homeowner maintains the private lateral from the house to the public main. If a backup happens, the first question is which side of the connection the clog is on. A camera look through sewer camera inspection answers it.
