What is documented about Dove Mountain

Dove Mountain covers roughly 6,200 acres in the foothills northeast of central Marana. The Ritz-Carlton hotel and resort opened there on December 18, 2009, long after the first homes. Homes have been built across more than two decades.

That spread matters for plumbing. Two neighbors can have a heater, fixtures and shutoff valves of very different ages. A home from an early phase may be on its second heater, while a newer one may still have the original.

Find your own water hardness

Start with your water bill, which names your provider. Many providers publish an annual water quality report, and the Town of Marana's FAQ says several water providers operate within Town limits. Ask which unit the report uses. Hardness appears as ppm or as grains per gallon, and one grain is about 17.1 ppm.

General advice: hardware stores sell low-cost hardness test strips that give a rough reading at your tap. If the result is high, our water softener and filtration page explains what to consider before buying equipment.

Questions to settle before a plumbing project

  • Who is my water provider? Your bill will say.
  • Is my sewer public or a septic system? Confirm before any drain work.
  • Does my HOA review exterior work? Listing sites describe many Dove Mountain neighborhoods as HOA communities.
  • Does my home even use natural gas? Many homes in Marana are all-electric, so confirm before planning gas work.

Cold nights and outdoor lines
